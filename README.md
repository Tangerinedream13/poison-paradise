# Poisoned Paradise 🌴💉

## An AI Security and Data Poisoning Dating Experience

![Gemma and Rex at the Poisoned Paradise villa, with neon beach villa lights and a digital matchmaking overlay](./public/images/poison-paradise-hero)

_Gemma and Rex meet again in Paradise Island’s glamorous villa — but the algorithm that paired them is being used to pull off a much bigger con._

**Poisoned Paradise** is an interactive choose-your-own-adventure web experience that teaches AI security, data poisoning, and evidence verification through a tropical reality-dating-show-inspired story.

## Project Overview

Gemma arrives at the Paradise Island villa hoping to find love, ready for sun, romance, and a fresh start. Instead, she discovers the Matchmaker keeps pairing her with Rex and pushing suspiciously specific trust questions about his work, passwords, routines, and security habits.

Gemma eventually realizes the romance is a social-engineering operation. She tells Rex the truth, he struggles to trust her, and together they decide to play along by feeding the operative fake credentials, fabricated Cyber Steel project names, false vulnerabilities, and traceable decoy files.

By the final episode, the operative takes the bait and Cyber Steel’s monitoring exposes their identity and location. The real reversal is that Gemma and Rex were never repeating the old betrayal — they were turning it back on the attacker.

The story keeps the flirty, dramatic personality of **Sexy-Phish** while leaning into tropical reality-show chaos, manipulated evidence, and the lingering question: who is controlling the match, and who is being controlled?

## What Learners Explore

- Data poisoning
- Digital evidence verification
- AI overreliance
- Source credibility
- Trust and manipulation in recommendation systems
